## 🌱 A little about me

I'm a graduate student at the **College of Pharmacy, Chung-Ang University**, with a **B.S. in Biology from SUNY Oswego**. I bring laboratory experience, Python, and machine learning together to explore biomedical questions and build useful software.

I want to turn advances in AI into **practical systems for pharmaceutical and biomedical work**. Along the way, I hope to help researchers feel more comfortable using AI by making its capabilities, evidence, and limitations easier to understand.
